fitbridge's first public beta connects Android Health Connect to ChatGPT and Claude through your own Cloudflare Worker.
Your health records stay in your Cloudflare account until you ask your assistant to read them.

This release adds browser-based passkey setup, a private Life Dashboard pairing QR, recovery and phone-key rotation, signed-sync status, and redacted support diagnostics.
The repository includes a Deploy to Cloudflare button, pinned CI, dependency updates, additive migrations, and documentation.

The original Android flow was tested by the owner.
The changed setup is covered by automated Chromium WebAuthn and synthetic ingestion tests.
A new physical-device trial of the pairing UI and a measured ten-minute setup trial remain outstanding.
iOS and additional wearable sources are experimental.

Read the setup guide and release-readiness evidence before importing long history.
This beta is for general wellness and is not a medical device.
